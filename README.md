# EU Cyber Resilience Act — Context Pack

**Regulation (EU) 2024/2847** of the European Parliament and of the Council of 23 October 2024 on horizontal cybersecurity requirements for products with digital elements (Cyber Resilience Act). CELEX: **32024R2847**. ELI: `http://data.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/2847/oj`.

This repository parses the consolidated CRA text into stable-ID legal units (e.g. `CRA-ART-14-PARA-2`) so that any recital, article, paragraph, point, or annex sub-division can be cited precisely. It was originally produced as a set of Claude artifacts and is now committed here as plain Markdown.

## ⚠️ Sourcing caveat — read before relying on this for compliance

The underlying text was **parsed from a third-party GitHub mirror** of the Official Journal PDF (`raw.githubusercontent.com/cra-compliance-lab/awesome-cra-compliance/main/docs/eu/cra-regulation-2024-2847.pdf`), **not fetched live from EUR-Lex** — EUR-Lex was unreachable from the sandbox this was produced in, and GitHub's raw-content CDN was the only reachable channel at the time. As a result:

- This corresponds to the **20.11.2024 consolidated snapshot** — the earliest possible consolidated version of the text.
- **Two known corrigenda** (dated 2024-12-05 and 2025-07-02) exist against CELEX 32024R2847 and were **never checked against this text**. Any provision may be affected by a corrigendum that is not reflected here.
- The **paragraph, point, and chapter boundaries** used to derive stable IDs are a mechanical parse of the printed structure and **have not had legal review**.

**Anyone relying on a specific provision for compliance purposes should verify the exact wording against [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R2847) directly**, rather than relying solely on this repository.
