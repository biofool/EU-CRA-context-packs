# Executive summary — EU CRA Context Packs

This project helps somatic studios and movement educators understand the EU Cyber Resilience Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/2847). It splits the consolidated regulation into small, stable-ID units so any article, paragraph, point, recital, or annex can be located quickly.

## Why this matters for somatic studios

Studios and field practitioners use many products with digital elements: booking apps, payment terminals, wearable sensors, streaming cameras, smart lighting, mobile apps, and remote-data services. The CRA sets cybersecurity, vulnerability-handling, support-period, and conformity obligations on the manufacturers of those products. Studios are usually end users, not manufacturers, but the regulation still affects what studios should expect from vendors.

This context pack helps studios to:

- Ask vendors the right questions: secure-by-default configuration, patch availability, support period, and incident notification.
- Recognise when a product may be classed as "important" or "critical" under the CRA.
- Check whether a device, app, or cloud service falls inside or outside the regulation's scope.
- Read the exact wording of exclusions for spare parts, free and open-source software, national security, and other carve-outs.

## What the repository provides

- Precise citation of the consolidated CRA text using stable identifiers.
- 843 indexed units across 130 recitals, 8 chapters, and 8 annexes.
- Plain Markdown files that work in any text editor, browser, or documentation tool.

## Important limits

This is not legal advice. The text was parsed from a third-party PDF mirror, not fetched live from EUR-Lex. Two corrigenda exist that were not checked against this copy. Always verify the exact wording on [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32024R2847) before making compliance decisions.
